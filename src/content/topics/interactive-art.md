en: |
  Notes on artworks and exhibitions that are completed by the movement and participation of visitors, seen from an HCI perspective. The focus is on input methods, the immediacy of response, experience through space and the body, and interaction among visitors.

  Based on Paul Dourish's concept of embodied interaction, works by teamLab, Random International, Refik Anadol and others, along with exhibitions in Korea and abroad, are analyzed.

관람자의 움직임과 참여로 완성되는 작품과 전시를 HCI 관점에서 정리합니다. 입력 방식, 반응의 즉각성, 공간과 신체를 통한 경험, 관람자 사이의 상호작용을 중심으로 봅니다.

Paul Dourish의 체화된 상호작용(embodied interaction) 개념을 바탕으로, teamLab, Random International, Refik Anadol 등의 작업과 국내외 전시를 분석합니다.
